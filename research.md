My research focuses on, **but is not limited to**, the interaction between structured light, complex optical systems, and quantum photonics. I am particularly interested in understanding how optical fields are transformed, how their geometrical and topological structure can be revealed and controlled, and how these ideas can be used to improve photonic technologies and quantum information systems.

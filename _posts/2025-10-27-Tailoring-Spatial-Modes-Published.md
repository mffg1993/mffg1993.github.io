title: "Published in Physical Review A: Tailoring Spatial Modes with Stimulated Parametric Down-Conversion"
---

Our work **"Tailoring spatial modes produced by stimulated parametric down-conversion"** has been published in *Physical Review A*.

We theoretically and experimentally study how the spatial properties of the pump and seed beams determine the modes produced through stimulated parametric down-conversion, providing control over the generated beam size, propagation, and modal fidelity.

[Read the published article](https://doi.org/10.1103/g22l-cgr1)

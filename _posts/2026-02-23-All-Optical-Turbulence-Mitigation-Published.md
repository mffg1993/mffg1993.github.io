Our work **"All-optical turbulence mitigation for free-space quantum key distribution using stimulated parametric down-conversion"** has been published in *Optica*.

We demonstrate an all-optical approach to mitigating turbulence-induced spatial-mode distortions using the phase-conjugation properties of stimulated parametric down-conversion. The scheme improves the robustness of high-dimensional free-space quantum key distribution under turbulent conditions.

[Read the published article](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPTICA.583778)

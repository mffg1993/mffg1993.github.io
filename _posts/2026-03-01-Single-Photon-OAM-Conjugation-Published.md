title: "Published in JOSA B: Single-Photon Orbital Angular Momentum Conjugation"
---

Our work **"Description of single-photon orbital angular momentum conjugation with stimulated parametric down-conversion"** has been published in the *Journal of the Optical Society of America B*.

In this invited article, we study how stimulated parametric down-conversion can be used to realize phase conjugation of orbital angular momentum at the single-photon level, and discuss a cascaded down-conversion scheme for identifying the stimulated contribution through heralded coincidence measurements.

[Read the published article](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAB.578036)

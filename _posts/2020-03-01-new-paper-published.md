I am delighted to share that I have joined Professor Jeff Lundeen’s Quantum Photonics Group at the University of Ottawa as a Postdoctoral Fellow. My work focuses on advancing experimental and theoretical approaches to structured light, quantum state measurement, and optical transformations. I am excited to contribute to the group’s pioneering research in quantum photonics and to collaborate within a vibrant community at the forefront of optical science.
